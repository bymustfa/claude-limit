# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the work.
Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted in issues, pull requests or any other
project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour bothers you, contact the maintainer,
[@bymustfa](https://github.com/bymustfa), through GitHub. Reports are handled privately. The
maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads or block people who break these rules.
